          Last updated October 2, 2026. Green cards 1820–FY2024; by country and class from FY2005. In fiscal 2024, 1,364,090 people became lawful permanent residents.

          Source: DHS Office of Homeland Security Statistics, Yearbook of Immigration Statistics. Counts persons granted lawful permanent resident status (a green card) in each fiscal year, whether they arrived on an immigrant visa or adjusted status from inside the country. By-country figures cover family, employment and diversity classes; refugees, asylees and smaller classes are shown only in totals. DHS rounds every cell to the nearest 10, so totals may not add exactly. Fiscal years run October to September.
