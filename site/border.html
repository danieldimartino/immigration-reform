      Where encounters happen

      Border Patrol sectors (between ports) and CBP field offices (at ports), for the same two fiscal years.

      Last updated October 7, 2026. Monthly data from October 1999 through August 2026; annual Border Patrol apprehensions from fiscal 1925. 24,379,673 encounters in the monthly series.

      Source: U.S. Customs and Border Protection, Nationwide Encounters. Encounters are events, not people: one person caught crossing twice counts twice. They combine Border Patrol apprehensions between ports of entry with Office of Field Operations inadmissibles at ports; "Air, sea and other" covers airports, seaports and coastal Border Patrol sectors. Title 42 public-health expulsions (March 2020 to May 2023) are excluded throughout. CBP names only its most common nationalities; the rest are grouped as "All other countries". The latest months are preliminary and CBP may revise them. Figures before fiscal 2020 are Border Patrol apprehensions from CBP's historical tables, with port-of-entry inadmissibles from the DHS Yearbook.

      Files used:

      • CBP Nationwide Encounters, current file and the FY2020–FY2023 file (monthly, by sector, nationality and demographic)
      • U.S. Border Patrol Total Apprehensions (FY 1925 – FY 2020) (CBP PDF, extracted once)
      • U.S. Border Patrol Monthly Encounters (FY 2000 – FY 2020) (CBP PDF, extracted once)
      • DHS Yearbook Table 36, CBP OFO encounters (port-of-entry inadmissibles FY2005–FY2019; FY2023 edition)

      Where CBP's older and newer files overlap, the newer (revised) figures are used. Title 42 began in March 2020, so the historical Border Patrol counts before fiscal 2020 contain no expulsions and the two eras are comparable.

      Compiled by Daniel Di Martino. Fiscal years run October to September.
